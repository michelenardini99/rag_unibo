Students from the Università di Bologna  who want to attend the programme have to be regularly enrolled  at  the  degree  programme  Laurea  Magistrale  in  'Psicologia  Clinica'  or  at  the  Laurea Magistrale  in  'Psicologia  Scolastica  e  di  Comunità',  must  present  a  valid  certification  of  their knowledge of the Spanish language (B1 level) and must be entitled of a valid visa or residence permit. In August, in their third semester of study (correspondent to the second semester of the fourth year of the Programa), students shall be transferred to Universidad del Rosario in order to attend the teaching activities offered within the 'Programa de Psicología' in accordance with the correspondence table annexed to the present agreement (Annex 1). Students must obtain respectively a minimum of 16 or 17 Colombian credits depending on their path as provided in the correspondence table. Credits earned at Universidad del Rosario consist in a workload equivalent to at least 30 Italian credits even if they are recognised respectively as 26 or 28 Italian credits due to Università di Bologna study plan structure. During the mobility students from the Università di Bologna will have the state exam to obtain the professional degree in psychology in Colombia, which consists in a written test. The cost of this test is charged to the students. Programa de Psicología is responsible for enrolling the student to the state test beforehand and according to the state exam calendars.
Finally, they will return to the Università di Bologna to complete their studies including the 'Prova Pratica Valutativa (PPV)' necessary for the professional habilitation of Psychologist in Italy and to obtain the Università di Bologna degree qualification.
